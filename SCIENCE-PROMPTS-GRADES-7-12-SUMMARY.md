## Prompt Structure

Each teaching prompt includes the following comprehensive sections:

### 1. **PREREQUISITES** (3-4 items)
- Prior knowledge required
- Foundational concepts
- Skills needed
- Connection to previous learning

### 2. **LEARNING OBJECTIVES** (4 items)
- Clear, measurable outcomes
- Action verbs (define, apply, analyze, evaluate)
- Progressive cognitive levels
- Aligned with standards

### 3. **KEY CONCEPTS** (4-5 items)
- Core ideas and principles
- Essential vocabulary
- Relationships between concepts
- Real-world applications
- Connections to other topics

### 4. **COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS** (4 items)
- Student misunderstandings
- Oversimplifications
- Confusion with related concepts
- Incorrect assumptions from experience

### 5. **TEACHING SEQUENCE** (30 minutes total)

#### HOOK (3 minutes)
- Engaging question or demonstration
- Real-world connection
- Surprising fact or phenomenon
- Curiosity spark

#### DIRECT INSTRUCTION (10-12 minutes)
- Systematic concept presentation
- Visual aids and models
- Clear examples and non-examples
- Prior knowledge connections
- Real-world applications
- Understanding checks

#### GUIDED PRACTICE (8-10 minutes)
- Structured activities with support
- Concept application to examples
- Reasoning and strategy discussion
- Immediate feedback
- Collaborative learning

#### INDEPENDENT PRACTICE (6-9 minutes)
- Independent demonstration
- Application to new situations
- Exercises and problem-solving
- Creative applications
- Self-assessment

### 6. **DIFFERENTIATION**

#### For Struggling Learners:
- Graphic organizers and visual aids
- Breaking complex concepts into steps
- Concrete before abstract
- Additional practice with feedback
- Scaffolded support

#### For Advanced Learners:
- Advanced applications
- Current research connections
- Experimental design
- Interdisciplinary projects
- Extended investigations

### 7. **ASSESSMENT INDICATORS** (4 items)
- Observable evidence of understanding
- Skill demonstrations
- Concept applications
- Communication of reasoning
